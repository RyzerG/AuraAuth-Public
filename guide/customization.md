# Interface & Customization

AuraAuth offers a deeply customizable interface to suit your visual preferences and privacy needs.

## Vault Layouts
Toggle between layouts instantly using the Grid/List icon in the top header:

* **List View**: A classic, high-density vertical stack. Best for users with many accounts who prefer reading full email addresses.
* **Grid View**: A modern, immersive 2-column grid featuring massive circular progress indicators and prominent brand logos.

## Privacy Shield
When using AuraAuth in public spaces (e.g., coffee shops, public transit), you can obscure your codes to prevent shoulder-surfing.

1. Tap the **Eye** icon floating in the bottom right corner.
2. All 6-digit codes will instantly be masked with privacy dots (`••• •••`).
3. **You can still tap the masked cards to copy the codes to your clipboard.**

## Fancy Graphics Mode
AuraAuth includes an optional high-fidelity rendering mode. This can be toggled via the **Settings** gear icon.

**When Fancy Graphics is ON:**
* **Liquid Glass Cards**: Account tiles transform into translucent slate-glass with a 3D ice-blue stroke.
* **Ambient Particles**: A custom canvas engine renders floating, glowing bokeh spheres that drift through the background.

**When Fancy Graphics is OFF:**
* The app reverts to a pure black-and-grey aesthetic with flat, solid borders for maximum battery efficiency and OLED power saving.
