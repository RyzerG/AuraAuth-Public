# Fancy Graphics Mode

Fancy Graphics replaces standard flat card rendering with high-depth translucent materials and floating particle simulation.

## Visual Enhancements

* **Frosted Glass Styling**: Increases card opacity using a slate-glass foundation (`#331E293B`) paired with an ice-blue border outline.
* **Particle Canvas**: Renders 7 floating ambient glass orbs in the background running on a hardware-accelerated render loop.
* **Zero Gyroscope Jitter**: Operates on fixed time interpolation rather than sensor hardware, preventing UI shake during active device handling.

## Toggling Fancy Graphics

1. Tap the **Settings** gear icon in the top header.
2. Toggle the **Fancy Graphics** switch.
3. Preferences persist locally in your configuration.
